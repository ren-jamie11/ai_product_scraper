    Amazon

    Listings and reviews are both read from this site, for new extractions. Reviews come straight from Amazon with your saved sign-in, taken evenly across star ratings; every one is tagged, so more reviews means a longer, costlier parse.

    Models & reasoning

    Tagging makes one call per text body, so it runs on a cheaper model. Grouping makes one call per tag list and is pure judgement, so it's worth more. Changes apply to the next run, never to one already in flight.
