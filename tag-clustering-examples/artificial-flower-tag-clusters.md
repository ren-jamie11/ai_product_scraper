### Grouping Notes
- Separated what the flowers are made of (Silk & Plastic Materials) from how they look (Realistic, Natural Look) and from how long they hold up (Durability; Lasting Look Season After Season).
- Split weather protection from fade protection: tags naming rain, wind, water, or "weather" went to Weatherproof for Outdoors, even when they also mention UV; tags about UV and fading only went to UV & Fade Resistance. These two could be merged into one "Outdoor Weather & UV Resistance" group if a shorter list is preferred.
- Kept "Durability & Build Quality" (physically sturdy) apart from "Lasting Look Season After Season" (keeps its shape, fullness, and freshness over time), since the second is about appearance lasting, not toughness.
- "realistic color" → Vibrant, Colorful Blooms rather than Realistic, Natural Look, because its subject is color; likewise "vibrant silk petals" went to color rather than materials.
- "bright color that resists fading and wilting" → UV & Fade Resistance, and "realistic stems and leaves add fullness" → Full, Lush Blooms, because each tag's stated benefit is fade resistance and fullness respectively.
- "low-maintenance fall color" → No Watering or Upkeep, not Fall Colors, because the benefit it states is low maintenance; "perpetually fresh autumn decor" → Fall Colors & Autumn Décor rather than Lasting Look, because its subject is seasonal decorating.
- Container fit (Fits Vases, Planters & Baskets) is kept apart from stem flexibility (Bendable, Shapeable Stems); "flexible stems fit vases, baskets, and planters" went with containers because the benefit is fit. "petite mums fit walker baskets" also went with containers, while "petite mums" alone went to Compact Size.
- Input notes: "pot not included" is a neutral disclosure rather than a benefit, and "easy to use" is too vague to pair with anything, so it stands alone. "water-free semi-realistic look" is a qualified realism claim, grouped with realism and reflected in the description's "from a distance" caveat.

### Grouping Notes
- Grouped by what went wrong for the customer: appearance falling short, size falling short, a finishing defect, a weak part, and a limit on customizing.
- Kept "looks fake up close" apart from "doesn't look like sunflowers": the first is about realism in general, the second is about not matching the expected flower type (closer to "not as advertised").
- "does not fill 12 x 12 inch pots" → Smaller Than Expected, because the root problem is size and fullness falling short of expectations, not a pot-specific fit issue.
- "excess plastic on leaves from manufacturing" is kept as its own finishing defect rather than merged into Look Fake Up Close; it could be merged there if leftover plastic is treated as a cause of the fake look.
- Kept the weak part ("stem pieces droop") separate from the customizing limit ("fern pieces cannot be removed"), since one is a quality problem and the other is a design limitation.
- Input notes: ": not the most realistic up close" starts with a stray colon, kept verbatim for traceability. The complaints partly echo the feature list's "realistic from a distance" and "fills a medium-sized vase" tags, which suggests those qualifiers are worth keeping in listing copy.
