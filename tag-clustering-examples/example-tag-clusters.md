## Notes

- **Possible SKU contamination in the feature set.** "fits standard pillow inserts" and "easy seasonal sofa decorating" appear to come from a pillow listing; the topiary and tree tags suggest a few non-mum SKUs were included in the source data. Consider filtering before using this as a keyword base.
- **"lightweight plastic pot" appears in both sets.** Lightweight reads as a portability benefit indoors and a stability failure outdoors — worth splitting in copy by use case.
- **Pot weight is the highest-leverage product fix.** Complaint clusters 7 and 8 share a root cause, and they mirror the positives in feature cluster 10 (weighted cement pot, stones/gravel for stability).
