### Grouping Notes
- Separated closely related benefits that shoppers weigh differently: general durability vs. chip/scratch resistance, heat retention vs. cool-touch handles, easy cleaning vs. stain/odor resistance, and satisfying heft vs. lightweight.
- Split size into distinct needs: large capacity, a generally pleasing size, the right everyday size for coffee/tea, room for cream and add-ins, coffee-machine fit, and compact size, because each answers a different shopping question.
- Tags that combine two attributes were placed by the attribute stated first or most specific: "great size and weight" → Great Overall Size; "lightweight and comfortable to hold" → Lightweight; "large capacity for coffee and cream" → Large Size & Capacity rather than Room for Cream; "strong and heat-resistant" → Durability rather than Stays Cool.
- "Colors and patterns" tags went to Variety of Patterns, "colors and design/details" tags to Attractive Design, and "color and texture" tags to Textured Surface ("consistent color and texture" went to Glaze & Finish because it describes glaze consistency).
- Base tags that name both stability and heat ("wide base retains heat and provides balance," "wide bottom and narrow top for stability and keeping beverages warm") went to Stable, Non-Slip Base because the base shape is the subject.
- "food-grade thick porcelain" → Lead-Free & Non-Toxic (food-grade safety) rather than Quality Ceramic; "non-porous glazed porcelain ceramic" → Quality Ceramic (material) rather than Stain & Odor Resistant.
- Ambiguous placements: "no metal holder needed" (likely glass-style cups that would otherwise need a heat holder) → Stays Cool to the Touch; "tall thin mugs fit more in dishwasher" → Stackable & Space-Saving (space efficiency); "unique hand-painted boho pattern" → Hand-Painted rather than Style Themes.
- Handle tags were split three ways (comfort, handle size, accessibility for weak/arthritic hands) since accessibility is a distinct selling point; these could be merged if fewer groups are preferred.

### Grouping Notes
- Grouped by the root problem the customer experienced rather than the specific attribute, so a color mismatch and a capacity shortfall ("does not hold 16 oz") share one Not as Described group.
- Kept "the handle is awkward" separate from "the mug is heavy," since one points to handle design and the other to overall weight.
- "mug requires two hands to hold" → Hard-to-Hold Handles rather than Heavier Than Wanted, because it most directly describes a grip problem; it could also reflect weight, so these two groups could be merged if the source reviews link them.
- "less attractive cup painting" was kept apart from Not as Described because it states a judgment about looks, not a mismatch with the listing.
- "cups may crack in microwave" is hedged ("may"), so it may be a caution rather than a reported defect; it was kept as its own durability/safety problem. It also contradicts microwave-safe claims in the features list.
- "does not keep coffee hot" directly contradicts the Keeps Drinks Hot feature group, which is worth checking against the source listings.
