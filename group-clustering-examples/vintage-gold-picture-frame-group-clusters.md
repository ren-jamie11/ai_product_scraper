### Grouping Notes
- Themes are separated by customer need: where and how the frame displays, what protects the photo, overall style, decorative detail, how solidly it's built, what it's made of, loading photos, what's in the box, and what it fits.
- Style and detailing are kept apart: overall look and finish (vintage, antique gold, European) vs. specific carved ornamentation (floral, leaf, wavy edges). They merge cleanly into one 31-tag appearance theme.
- "Lightweight, Durable Build" goes with materials, not "Sturdy, Well-Made Build," because the build theme's tags stress heaviness and a lightweight member would contradict it. Merging build and materials would give one 20-tag theme.
- "Easel Stands & Hanging Hooks" joins the display theme because the hardware exists to enable wall and tabletop placement; the single-tag "Wall, Tabletop & Dual-Orientation Display" bridges the placement and orientation groups, so all four sit together.
- "Thick Plastic Cover" sits with the glass groups because it serves the same protective-front need; the theme could split into "Real, Fixed Glass" (7 tags) and "Protective, Clear Display" (9 tags) if material vs. benefit matters.
- "Easy to Clean" has no component named, so it joins the front-cover theme as the closest upkeep-related benefit; it could also move to Included Extras (6 tags) or stand alone.
- "Stable Photo Positioning" is placed by its benefit (photos stay put once loaded) with photo loading, not with the easel/display theme.
- Mats and the trimming template go with included extras, kept apart from the size/proportion theme; moving the template there would give a 5-tag size theme.
- Source note: "Durability & Sturdy Build" is labeled Tags (10) but lists 11 tags; the stated count is used here.
- All 29 groups (104 tags) are assigned to exactly one theme.
