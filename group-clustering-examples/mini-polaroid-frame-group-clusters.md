### Grouping Notes
- Merged groups by the customer need they serve (where it displays, what it's made of, how it looks, what fits, how photos load, what protects them) rather than by the component involved.
- Kept one combined display theme instead of splitting magnetic from tabletop, because "Magnetic or Tabletop Display" explicitly bridges the two and "Portrait & Landscape Display" depends on the tabletop stands. If the 76-tag size is too broad for downstream use, "Secure Magnetic Display" is the natural piece to split off.
- Separated what the wood *is* and how solidly it's built (Theme 2) from how it *looks* (Theme 4). "Durable, Sturdy Build" went with the wood construction because its tags repeatedly reference wood ("thick wood frame," "substantial wood construction").
- Placed "Refined Edge Details" under looks rather than build, since beveled and rounded edges are described as design details; its "thick frame" wording is secondary.
- Split photo sizing (which photos fit, Theme 5) from mats (how the opening is adjusted, Theme 6), even though interchangeable mats also serve photo-size flexibility, because mats are a distinct included part customers evaluate on their own.
- Put "Snug, Edge-Safe Photo Fit" with photo loading and retention rather than sizing, because its tags describe how firmly photos are held, not which photo sizes fit.
- Merged acrylic and real glass fronts into one protective-front theme; they are opposing material choices, so keep the member groups visible if the acrylic-vs-glass distinction matters for copy.
- All 27 groups (238 tags) are assigned to exactly one theme.
