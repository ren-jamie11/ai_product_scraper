# Group-of-Groups: Wooden Serving Bowls

## Product Features & Benefits

### 1. Solid, Handcrafted Wood Build
Bowls are handmade from natural mango or acacia wood and are thick, heavy, and durable enough for long-lasting use.

**Groups (3) · Tags (24):**
- Durability & Sturdy Build (15)
- Handmade Craftsmanship (5)
- Natural Wood Construction (4)

### 2. Generous Size for Salads & Groups
Bowls are deep and roomy enough to serve salads to families and groups, while still fitting on a kitchen counter.

**Groups (3) · Tags (17):**
- Large Capacity for Groups (10)
- Suitable Size for Salads (5)
- Counter-Friendly Size (2)

### 3. Warm Grain & Attractive Design
Bowls show warm, varied natural wood grain in a stylish, unique design that widens toward the top.

**Groups (3) · Tags (12):**
- Natural Grain & Warm Color (8)
- Stylish, Unique Design (3)
- Widening Bowl Shape (1)

### 4. Smooth, Easy-Care Finish
Bowls have a smooth, food-grade, waterproof finish that rinses clean easily, resists odors and stains, and needs little maintenance.

**Groups (3) · Tags (9):**
- Easy, Low-Maintenance Care (4)
- Smooth Wood Finish (3)
- Food-Grade Waterproof Finish (2)

### 5. Versatile Everyday Serving
Bowls can toss and serve salads, pasta, fruit, and popcorn for daily meals and special events alike.

**Groups (2) · Tags (5):**
- Versatile Food Serving (4)
- Everyday & Special-Event Use (1)

### 6. Decor Accent & Gift Appeal
Bowls double as decorative centerpieces and make versatile, thoughtful gifts.

**Groups (2) · Tags (4):**
- Decorative Centerpiece Use (2)
- Versatile, Thoughtful Gift (2)

### 7. Utensils Included, Well Packaged
Bowls come with matching serving spoons and forks and arrive well packaged for shipping.

**Groups (2) · Tags (4):**
- Matching Serving Utensils Included (3)
- Protective Shipping Packaging (1)

### Grouping Notes
- Themes separate what the bowl is made of and how solidly it's built (1) from how it looks (3) and how its surface feels and cleans (4); material, look, and finish are distinct needs even though all three involve wood.
- "Handmade Craftsmanship" went into the build theme because its tags lead with "handmade premium wood construction"; two of its tags mention appearance, so it could move to theme 3 instead (build 19 tags, look 17 tags).
- "Easy, Low-Maintenance Care" merged with the two finish groups because its tags credit the smooth surface for easy rinsing and stain resistance: the easy care depends on the finish.
- "Suitable Size for Salads" sits with size, not with serving versatility, because every tag is about the bowl's dimensions rather than what foods it handles.
- "Widening Bowl Shape" (1 tag) folded into the look theme as a design detail rather than standing alone.
- "Decorative Centerpiece Use" and "Versatile, Thoughtful Gift" are both non-dining uses; either could merge into theme 5 for a single 9-tag "Versatile Uses, Serving to Decor & Gifting" theme.
- Out-of-box extras (utensils and packaging) are grouped together as the ready-to-use theme.
- All 18 groups (75 tags) are assigned to exactly one theme.
