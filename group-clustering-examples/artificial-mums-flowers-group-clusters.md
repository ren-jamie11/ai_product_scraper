# Group-of-Groups: Artificial Mums Flowers

## Product Features & Benefits

### 1. Full, Easily Shaped Arrangements
Dense blooms and foliage fill out into full, rounded arrangements once the flexible, wired stems are fluffed and shaped, and some flower heads can be detached and reattached.

**Groups (3) · Tags (30):**
- Full, Lush Arrangements (17)
- Easy Fluffing & Shaping (12)
- Detachable Flower Heads (1)

### 2. Weather-Resistant, Shed-Free Blooms
UV-treated, waterproof flowers resist fading, discoloration, wear, and heat or cold outdoors, and their blooms and leaves stay attached in wind and heavy rain.

**Groups (2) · Tags (26):**
- UV, Fade & Weather Resistance (24)
- Secure Blooms & Leaves (2)

### 3. Durable, Low-Maintenance Flowers
Well-made flowers hold their shape for several seasons, can be reused year after year, and need no watering, pruning, or sunlight, so they suit even low-light spaces.

**Groups (4) · Tags (26):**
- Durable, Well-Made Construction (13)
- No Watering or Upkeep (8)
- Reusable Seasonal Decor (4)
- Suitable for Low Light (1)

### 4. Attractive Colors & Fall Styling
Flowers come in attractive shades from white and soft gradients to vivid, cheerful mixes, including muted autumn palettes and pumpkin-and-plaid designs.

**Groups (2) · Tags (26):**
- Colorful, Attractive Blooms (18)
- Fall Colors & Styling (8)

### 5. Realistic, Detailed Blooms
Flowers and greenery look natural, especially from a short distance, with well-shaped heads, layered petals, detailed centers, and soft silk-like or chenille textures.

**Groups (3) · Tags (25):**
- Realistic Flowers & Greenery (16)
- Detailed, Well-Shaped Blooms (5)
- Soft, Textured Decor (4)

### 6. Ready-to-Display Sets
Flowers arrive pre-potted, fully assembled, and securely packaged, with generous flower quantities, matching pots or bases, and included adhesive hooks.

**Groups (4) · Tags (22):**
- Pre-Potted & Fully Assembled (12)
- Complete Sets & Included Pieces (7)
- Reinforced Shipping Packaging (2)
- Included Adhesive Hooks (1)

### 7. Silk, Fabric & Plastic Materials
Flowers combine silk and fabric blossoms with plastic, PE, or rubber leaves and wire-reinforced stems, and have little or no lingering odor.

**Groups (3) · Tags (17):**
- Silk, Plastic, Rubber & Wire (11)
- Small, Thick Fabric Blossoms (3)
- Low or No Odor (3)

### 8. Versatile, Easy Placement
Lightweight arrangements are easy to move and place straight from the box, suit indoor and outdoor spaces, and are sized for mantels, outdoor decor, and standard pillow inserts.

**Groups (5) · Tags (15):**
- Indoor & Outdoor Display (4)
- Lightweight & Easy to Move (4)
- Easy Placement & Decorating (3)
- Versatile Display Size (3)
- Standard Pillow Insert Fit (1)

### 9. Sturdy, Functional Pots
Pots offer weighted, tip-resistant stability, decorative finishes, foam fill for adding extra items, and drainage holes for rainwater.

**Groups (4) · Tags (13):**
- Sturdy, Tip-Resistant Pots (6)
- Decorative Pot Designs (3)
- Foam-Filled Pots (3)
- Rainwater Drainage Holes (1)

### Grouping Notes
- Themes separate what the product is made of (materials), how long it lasts with little effort (durability and upkeep), how it survives outdoor exposure (weather), and how it looks (color vs. realism); weather resistance and build durability merge cleanly into one 52-tag theme if you prefer a single longevity theme.
- Dependent groups merge: fullness comes from fluffing and shaping, so "Full, Lush Arrangements" and "Easy Fluffing & Shaping" form one theme, with "Detachable Flower Heads" folded in as another way to configure the arrangement.
- Borderline groups go by stated benefit: "Secure Blooms & Leaves" joins weather because its tags cite wind and rain; "Suitable for Low Light" joins low-maintenance because the upkeep tags already say no sunlight is needed; "Low or No Odor" joins materials as a material-quality trait.
- A member must not contradict its theme: "Lightweight & Easy to Move" goes under placement, not with the weighted, tip-resistant pots.
- "Easy Placement & Decorating" goes under placement (how it is used) rather than ready-to-display (what arrives in the box); moving it there makes theme 6 a 25-tag theme and theme 8 a 12-tag theme.
- Pot groups stay as their own theme because stability and pot design are a distinct need; they could merge into "Ready-to-Display Sets" as included containers for a 35-tag theme.
- "Standard Pillow Insert Fit" and the chenille tags in "Soft, Textured Decor" appear to come from a fall pillow cover rather than mums, so they were parked in the closest size and texture themes; consider removing them from the source data.
- All 30 groups (200 tags) are assigned to exactly one theme.
