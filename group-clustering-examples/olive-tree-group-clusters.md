### Grouping Notes
- Split realism into overall impression (Theme 6) and specific component details (Theme 2), because customers praise "looks real from a distance" differently from "textured bark" or "translucent olives." The two could be merged into one 68-tag realism theme if a single appearance theme is preferred.
- Put "Attractive, Varied Colors" with realistic details rather than a separate look theme, since its tags stress natural, varied, not-overly-bright tones that serve realism.
- Merged shaping and fullness (Theme 1) because the fullness tags repeatedly depend on fluffing and shaping ("full foliage after fluffing," "full shape after shaping branches").
- Placed "Secure, Seamless Branch Connections" with assembly rather than sturdy build, because its tags describe how the sections join during setup.
- Placed "Secure, Shed-Free Foliage" with long-lasting greenery rather than build, because its stated benefit is no shedding, wilting, or falling over time.
- Placed "Secure Protective Packaging" and "Easy-to-Clean Basket" with the included planters, since both describe the out-of-box package and its container rather than the tree.
- Put "Lightweight Design" under placement rather than build, because its stated benefit is easier handling and placement, and it would contradict the heavy-base emphasis in Theme 5.
- Kept "Suitable for Low-Light Spaces" under placement rather than no-upkeep, because its tags describe where the tree can go, though it overlaps with "no sunlight needed."
- All 35 groups (283 tags) are assigned to exactly one theme.
