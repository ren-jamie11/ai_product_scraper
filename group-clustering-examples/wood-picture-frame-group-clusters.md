### Grouping Notes
- Separate what a frame is made of and how solidly it is built (Theme 1) from how the wood looks (Themes 2 and 5) and from its outline or profile (Theme 7).
- Place handcraftsmanship with construction, because its tags describe how the frame is made (handmade, hand-carved, traditional techniques) rather than a visual style.
- Place mixed-material groups by their lead material: "Real Wood & Glass Materials" joins wood construction because every tag leads with the wood; glass-only groups form their own theme.
- Keep "Lightweight Build" with construction, since its tags pair lightness with sturdiness rather than describing easier placement.
- Keep rustic, natural character (Theme 2) apart from general finish, color, and style (Theme 5); they merge into one 60-tag appearance theme if a single "looks" bucket is preferred, and Theme 7 (shapes) could join that as well for 74 tags.
- Put "Sleek Complementary Matting" under appearance, not photo fit, because its tag praises how the mat looks rather than how it sizes the opening.
- Fold single-tag groups into the closest need: polished glass edges go with glass, no-assembly setup goes with out-of-box packaging, and "Practical, Versatile Design" goes with photo fit.
- Theme 9 is small (7 tags) and could be folded into Theme 6 as one 23-tag "loading and fitting photos" theme.
- All 53 groups (203 tags) are assigned to exactly one theme.
