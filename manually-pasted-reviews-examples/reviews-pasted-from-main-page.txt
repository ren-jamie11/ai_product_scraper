5 out of 5 starsNice ambient glow, 4,6,8 hr timer , nice to look at. Thick glass lamp shade.
Reviewed in Canada on August 9, 2025
Color: Black Walnut
Vine Customer Review of Free Product
I received one candle warmer lamp with dark wood base.

This lamp is interesting. I like the no flame design.

The lamp looks good, the pole is matte black and the base is a dark wood in a rounded corner design.

The pole is adjustable so I can fit a small or large candle underneath, I used a large candle and it fit with some space above it. The glass lamp shade is thick and the outside of it is a dimpled design.

I turned on the lamp fully, to the highest heat. After 15 minutes the lamp shade (glass) gets hot, enough to be uncomfortable to touch. It melted the candle slowly but easily.

After switching heat level to low , after 10 minutes or so the glass cooled , though it still continued to be warm.

The pole did not heat up during that time.

It is suggested to keep it at certain heat levels depending on the size of your candle, the suggested heat levels are indicated in the information/use booklet.

There is a timer feature, 4, 6, 8 hours, if you forget it or fall asleep it will shut off automatically.

I think that's a good safety feature seeing how it can get pretty hot when going for a while.

It feels flat and steady on the table surface.

When lit up it has a nice glow to it, adding some nice ambient light to the room.

5 out of 5 starsWorks well and looks good!
Reviewed in Canada on August 17, 2025
Color: Black Walnut
Vine Customer Review of Free Product
This seems like a good quality construction. The glass is nice and thick, makes it look like it's made of crystal.


Assembly was straight forward, simply add the bulb that is included and adjust the height of the glass heater.


There are four heat settings that also adjust the brightness of the light. The brighter, the hotter. The candle I tested on had essential oils and it worked very well at the second-lowest setting. It woked very well to extract the scents from the candle and make the living room smell nice.


The in-line remote allows you to set a delayed off time of 2, 4 or 8 hours. After two hours, I found it was time to turn it off to allow the liquid part of the candle to re-harden.


Both an attractive piece in the living room, and a practical scent generating item, I would have no problem recommending this to anyone who enjoys candle scents
