Keyword patterns

Build a shape rather than a single word. A search term matches a pattern when it contains at least one keyword from every group, in any order. Type a keyword and press Enter. End a keyword with * to match by prefix, so gold* also catches golden. Flip a group to exclude to reject terms containing its keywords instead of requiring them. Drag a group by its handle onto another pattern to copy it.
